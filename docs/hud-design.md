# HUD design

The HUD for children aged 3–8, phone in portrait (800 × 1280). Pictures first, words only for children who read (the
age setting decides). Few, big tap targets that stay in the same place.

Chosen 2026‑11‑12 ("face is the button", from eleven mock-ups on the owner's private artifact page "Lightgame HUD
Variants"), then changed while building it. **Built** below is what the code does today (`ui.hud`); **Planned** is
still the target. The HUD is drawn at 4 pack pixels per pixel (`Hud.SCALE`), text at 3 (`ResourcesService.UI_SCALE`).

## Built

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Bottom centre | **The face** (`Portrait`, 184 × 184): Teemo from the pack's emotes in a dark wooden frame (`backdrops_c1`). Its idle follows stamina: awake (blinks, twitches an ear), tired (yawns), asleep. Any code can make it react for a while or once (`Portrait.play`, e.g. cheering). Tapped (or the stamina bar under it), it plays a gesture once: happy, cheering, an ear twitch or a blink, at random, never the same twice in a row, and not while it is busy with another reaction (`FaceReactions`). |
| Under the face | **Stamina** (`StaminaBar`): 14 segments, green for what is left, with a mood face (happy, neutral at half or less, sad at a quarter or less). The model is `Stamina` on the player (`use`, `fill`, `set`); opening a chest or time passing don't lower it yet. |
| Left of the face | **Backpack**: 2 slots showing what the player carries. Tapping a slot throws its item out next to the player (with nowhere for it to land, the slot shakes); next to an animal wishing for that item it gives it instead, as the animal's button does. With a full backpack, stepping onto an item throws out the first slot's item (the slot shakes) and takes the new one a second later. Taps on the bottom row's pieces (slots, face, stamina bar) stay in the HUD; taps in the gaps between them reach the map behind. |
| Right of the face | **The tool button** (the pack's brown round button, `ToolButton`): the tool for where the player stands (the scythe on grass: tapping it cuts the grass; the fishing rod on a fishing spot: tapping it casts, and it stays while the rabbit fishes; the door on the door the rabbit came out of: tapping it goes back through; a tool shows only over the hint); without one **the hint**, a question mark; tapping it makes the face cheer and say "play around!" in a bubble over it (`SpeechBubble`: the dialog box turned so its pointer faces down). Next to a cow wishing for a crop the player doesn't carry it says "find" with the crop's picture. What it should do later is open. |
| On the map, over a chest | **Action button** (`ActionButton`, the pack's dark brown round button, a bit smaller than the hint, on the UI stage, following what it acts on): next to a chest it appears over it, showing a gold star (treasure inside); tapping it opens the chest, which throws its items out onto free tiles. The chest stays open and, empty, has no button any more. |
| On the map, a fishing spot | **Fishing** (feature flag `FISHING`, `FishingFeature`): standing still on a fishing spot (Tiled's `FISHING_SPOT`), the tool button shows the fishing rod. Tapped, the rabbit casts to the left (the camera centres on it); the bobber drops into the water two tiles away with a splash and bobs for 3–10 s (at random), then a fish pulls it under: the rod shakes, the face says "!!!" (happy), and a paw points at the place, pulsing, next to a circle that empties in 1.2 s (the gear's hold circle backwards). Now and then (30 %) no fish comes: the bobber bobs the whole 10 s, the line comes out and the face is sad, as when a fish gets away. A tap anywhere in time opens the fishing game, a window in the middle (`ui.fishing.RhythmGame`, the settings panel without its title): three lanes of rippling water, a green line with a dark edge near their bottom (it pulses on the beat) in a slightly darker band (where a tap catches), and under each lane a round dark-brown button with a fishing net (a long handle, the hoop and its bag). About 12 fish (yellow, blue, a clownfish) swim down the lanes side-on, wiggling, in a random order of lanes, each reaching the line on a half-second beat (one beat or a few apart) within 8 s. A tap on a lane's net, or anywhere in its lane, while a fish of that lane is on the line catches it: it grows a little, turns a little green and is gone with a sparkle, the line flashes there, the face is happy. A fish that passes the line fades away and a pink X pops up on the left (from the bottom up), the face sad; three X lose at once. A tap with no fish on the line tangles that net for a moment: it shakes and goes dark, and catches nothing until then; tapped again meanwhile, the moment starts again (so tapping everything doesn't win); a second tap just after a catch in the same lane (a double tap) does nothing. Taps outside the panel do nothing. The window stays over the whole screen and the panel in its middle when the screen is turned. Won: the rabbit pulls the fish out, the face puts its sunglasses on, a fish of a random colour goes into the backpack (full: it lands next to the rabbit; no free tile there either: it isn't collected). Lost, or no tap after the bite: the face is sad. One difficulty number (`FishingFeature.setLevel`, 0.15 now) picks the rules (`RhythmRules.forLevel`): how fast the fish fall (2.2 / 1.6 / 1.2 s from the top to the line), how close to the line a tap catches (0.4 / 0.28 / 0.14 s), how long a net stays tangled (1.0 / 0.9 / 0.8 s), at the levels for 3-, 5- and 8-year-olds (0.15, 0.45, 0.8); the run's length, the beat, the number of fish and lanes and the misses that lose can be set too (`FishingFeature.setRules`). A thin line goes from the rod's tip to the bobber. |
| On the map, grass | **Grass, cut with the tool button** (feature flag `GROWING_GRASS`): standing still on grass, the tool button shows the scythe (no action button over the grass); tapping it makes the rabbit swing its scythe once (0.8 s) and the grass on its tile and on all eight tiles around gets smaller as it fades out. Grass sprouts every 30 s on tiles the rabbit can get to (0.1 %, 1 % next to grass; the map may put some on tiles, Tiled's `GRASS` with a `phase` 1–4) and grows like a crop: a tuft and two tufts under everyone, a bush and a big bush over them (90 % opaque), never over the HUD, the action buttons or the wish bubbles. It blocks nothing. |
| On the map, over a field | **The same action button**: over a ripe field it shows the crop; tapping it makes the crop jump out onto a free tile and leaves the field empty but for a tiny sprout (the same for every crop). After picking it shows the watering can: the rabbit waters the field (water falls onto it), it grows a phase at once and then by itself, a phase every 30 s (the plant bounces a little each time and slowly squashes and stretches while it grows), with no button meanwhile; ripe again, the button is back with the crop. The can never runs dry. A sign at the field's top left corner shows its crop; while the field is empty (picked, not yet watered) it shows a question mark instead. Picking a crop makes the face happy (its animation 3 times). Nobody chose what grows yet (no seeds), so after watering a weed may grow instead (40 %, Tiled's `weedChance`); the sign keeps its question mark, and the ripe weed's button pulls it out: it rises a little and fades out, nothing is gained, and the face sweats and says "Beautiful weed but unusable!" for 3 s. |
| On the map, over an animal | **Caring for it** (`CreatureFeature`), on action buttons in a row over the cow. A hand (the pack's paw) strokes it, also while it is hungry: it stands still while the hand fades out, then a heart over it (not covered by the button; the hand comes back only 20 s after a stroke), a small one (1.5× the pack heart) floats up from just over the player's ears. With several animals next to the player each button is for one of them and stays over it. When the cow wishes for a crop (a carrot, corn, a tomato, a pumpkin or a cucumber: one of its favourite dishes, at random), a second button with the crop (right of the hand) takes the wish bubble's place and gives it (the slot shakes, the cow eats and shows a heart); without that crop it shakes and the face says "find" with it. |
| On the map | **Wishes as picture bubbles** (`PictureBubble`): a small grey bubble with a picture over a character (drawn at 3 pack pixels per pixel, its picture as much smaller; a bubble near the top right may sit behind the gear, that is fine), in front of everyone on the map; the cow shows the crop it wishes for when it is hungry (`CowState.WISHING`; not fed for 90 s it gives up and grazes; chickens wish only for corn and give up after 15 s, pecking at the ground instead), hidden while the player is next to it (the action button with the carrot is there). Tapping the bubble walks the player next to the animal, as tapping the animal does. |
| Top right | **Settings gear** (`HoldButton`): opens only after a 3-second hold, while the pack's stamina circle fills at its top right. The panel (`SettingsPanel`) has only a title and a check mark to close. |

Collecting happens on the map: items lie on tiles, bobbing (`ItemOnGround`), and are picked up by walking onto them.

Changed from the first design: the action is a round button over the thing it acts on, not the face itself; stamina
is a bar, not apples; the day picture (sun, moon, weather) is left out until the game has a day; the backpack has 2
slots.

## Planned

In this order; each step its own branch, scenario tests through `GameDriver` (`e2e/README.md`).

1. **Settings.** Age (3–8, showing what it changes, e.g. "BUTTONS: PICTURE ONLY"), music, sounds. The age decides:
   words on buttons (readers only), talks on or off (off for 3–4), anything else age-dependent (see "Game modes by
   age" in `docs/ideas.md`).
2. **Talks (age 5+), no window.** The HUD slides down (only the gear stays) and the map darkens a little (×0.8). The
   other character's face on the left with its line in a speech bubble (`dialog_box`, text up to ~500 px wide); the
   player's face bottom right with 1–3 short answers in a row (`play_button/blank`, about 10 letters each, enforced by a
   check on the dialog data). A chosen answer and the previous line move up and fade. The last answer has the
   `continue_indicator` and ends the talk: the HUD comes back and a picture bubble over the character sums up what it
   wants. While a talk is open, map taps don't walk.
3. **Age 3–4 hint.** The tool button's hint "play around!" is a placeholder (next to a cow wishing for a crop it already
   says "find" with that crop): when a character wants something, tapping the
   hint shows a big arrow bouncing over the place to go, with a small picture of what is there. Open: what to do when
   that place is off screen (an arrow at the screen edge, or the camera gliding there and back).
4. **A real day.** Day, time and weather (the day picture next to the HUD, the time for readers), and stamina lowered
   by time and work, refilled by sleep.

Missing models for all this: dialog data (lines, 1–3 answers, which one ends, the summary
picture), the settings and where they are stored, a day clock and weather, portraits for every character that talks.
