# HUD design

The HUD for children aged 3–8, phone in portrait (800 × 1280). Pictures first, words only for children who read (the
age setting decides). Few, big tap targets that stay in the same place.

Chosen 2026‑11‑12 ("face is the button", from eleven mock-ups on the owner's private artifact page "Lightgame HUD
Variants"), then changed while building it. **Built** below is what the code does today (`ui.hud`); **Planned** is
still the target. The HUD is drawn at 4 pack pixels per pixel (`Hud.SCALE`), text at 3 (`ResourcesService.UI_SCALE`).

## Built

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Bottom centre | **The face** (`Portrait`, 184 × 184): Teemo from the pack's emotes in a dark wooden frame (`backdrops_c1`). Its idle follows stamina: awake (blinks, twitches an ear), tired (yawns), asleep. Any code can make it react for a while or once (`Portrait.play`, e.g. cheering). Tapped (or the stamina bar under it), it plays a gesture once: happy, cheering, an ear twitch or a blink, at random, never the same twice in a row, and not while it is busy with another reaction (`FaceReactions`). |
| Under the face | **Stamina** (`StaminaBar`): 14 segments, green for what is left, with a mood face (happy, neutral at half or less, sad at a quarter or less). The model is `Stamina` on the player (`use`, `fill`, `set`); opening a chest or time passing don't lower it yet. |
| Left of the face | **Backpack**: 3 slots (81 × 87, 8 apart, 20 from the face, 29 from the screen's left edge) showing what the player carries. Tapping a slot throws its item out next to the player (with nowhere for it to land, the slot shakes); next to an animal wishing for that item it gives it instead, as the animal's button does; while a swap is on offer (below) it chooses that slot's item to go instead, and nothing moves. With a full backpack, stepping onto an item leaves it where it lies: nothing jumps out of the backpack by itself. Taps on the bottom row's pieces (slots, face, stamina bar) stay in the HUD; taps in the gaps between them reach the map behind. |
| Beside the face | **Alignment**: the slots and the tool button (round or wide) have their middles on one line, the face's lower golden section (its height / φ², about 70 px above its bottom; `Hud.ROW_MIDDLE`); the backpack and the wide tool button are equally wide and equally far from the face and from the screen's edges; the gap to the face (20) is about the gap between slots (8) × φ², so the slots read as one group. |
| Right of the face | **The tool button** (the pack's brown round button, `ToolButton`): the tool for where the player stands (the scythe on grass: tapping it cuts the grass; the fishing rod on a fishing spot: tapping it casts, and it stays while the rabbit fishes; the door on the door the rabbit came out of: tapping it goes back through; a tool shows only over the hint); **the swap** (`SwapFeature`, before every tool, the scythe and the rod too): standing still on an item with the backpack full, the button widens to the left into the pack's rounded button (259 × 108, as wide as the backpack; its right edge, 29 from the screen's edge as the backpack on the left, and its middle stay): the backpack item that would go on its left, the swap arrows (drawn for the game: the pack has single arrows only) in the middle, the item on the ground on its right. The item that would go pulses, in its slot and on the button together (the same item), from its own colours to dark brown and back (1.2 s); the item on the ground doesn't; it is the left slot's at first, a tap on another slot chooses that one. Tapping the button swaps them: the item from the ground goes into that slot and the slot's item hops down where it lay, so the same tap swaps them back. Stepping off, the button is round again and the left slot comes first next time; without one **the hint**, a question mark; tapping it makes the face cheer and say "play around!" in a bubble over it (`SpeechBubble`: the dialog box turned so its pointer faces down). Next to a cow wishing for a crop the player doesn't carry it says "find" with the crop's picture. What it should do later is open. |
| On the map, over a chest | **Action button** (`ActionButton`, the pack's dark brown round button, a bit smaller than the hint, on the UI stage, following what it acts on): next to a chest it appears over it, showing a gold star (treasure inside); tapping it opens the chest, which throws its items out onto free tiles. The chest stays open and, empty, has no button any more. |
| On the map, a fishing spot | **Fishing** (feature flag `FISHING`, `FishingFeature`): standing still on a fishing spot (Tiled's `FISHING_SPOT`), the tool button shows the fishing rod. Tapped, the rabbit casts to the left (the camera centres on it once and stays); the bobber (three quarters of the pack's size) drops into the water two tiles away with a splash and bobs for 3–10 s (at random), then a fish pulls it under: the rod shakes, the face says "!!!" (happy), and a big hand pointer (the finger up, drawn for the game: the pack has only paws) just under the middle of the screen points up at it (the rabbit), pulsing, and is the clock too: white at first, it turns grey from its finger's tip down over the 1.2 s, row by row of its pixels. Now and then (30 %) no fish comes: the bobber bobs the whole 10 s, the line comes out and the face sweats (all that waiting). A tap anywhere in time opens the fishing game, a window in the middle of the screen above the HUD's bottom row, which stays bright so the face's reactions show (`ui.fishing.RhythmGame`, the settings panel without its title, 600 x 960): three lanes of rippling water, a green line with a dark edge near their bottom (it pulses on the beat) in a slightly darker band (where a tap catches), and under each lane a round dark-brown button with a fishing net (a long handle, the hoop and its bag). About 12 fish (yellow, blue, a clownfish) swim down the lanes side-on, wiggling, in a random order of lanes, each reaching the line on a half-second beat (one beat or a few apart) within 8 s. A tap on a lane's net, or anywhere in its lane, while a fish of that lane is on the line catches it: it grows a little, turns a little green and is gone with a sparkle, the line flashes there, the face is happy. A fish that passes the line swims on, turning red, and is gone at the water's bottom; a tap too early in a lane, while its next fish is coming but not on the line yet, scares that fish off: it turns red and jumps out of its lane to the side, a little up and then down, fading. Either is a miss: a pink X pops up on the left in one of the X shadows shown from the start (one per miss that loses, from the bottom up), the face sad; three X lose at once. A tap in a lane with no fish in sight only presses the net. Taps outside the panel do nothing. The window stays over the whole screen and the panel in its middle when the screen is turned. Won: the fish the player gets pops up big in the window's upper middle, with a little bounce and a gentle wobble, and sparkles of a few sizes (about twice as big as a chest's) burst out of it all around, flying out and fading; the window closes after 1.8 s (lost: 0.8 s); then the rabbit pulls the line out and the fish leaps out of the water where the bobber went under, flying to the rabbit in an arc, turning (0.7 s, `ui.fishing.CatchFlight`), the face puts its sunglasses on, and then the fish (of a random colour) goes into the backpack (full: it lands next to the rabbit; no free tile there either: it isn't collected). Lost, or no tap after the bite: the face is sad; with no tap after the bite a fish (yellow, blue or a clownfish, as in the game) also leaps out of the water where the bobber went under, almost straight up and head first, turns over at the top and drops back in head down, fading as it goes under (1.2 s, `ui.fishing.GotAwayLeap`). One difficulty number (`FishingFeature.setLevel`, 0.15 now) picks the rules (`RhythmRules.forLevel`): how fast the fish fall (2.2 / 1.6 / 1.2 s from the top to the line), how close to the line a tap catches (0.4 / 0.28 / 0.14 s), at the levels for 3-, 5- and 8-year-olds (0.15, 0.45, 0.8); the run's length, the beat, the number of fish and lanes and the misses that lose can be set too (`FishingFeature.setRules`). A thin line goes from the rod's tip to the bobber. |
| On the map, grass | **Grass, cut with the tool button** (feature flag `GROWING_GRASS`): standing still on grass, the tool button shows the scythe (no action button over the grass); tapping it makes the rabbit swing its scythe once (0.8 s) and the grass on its tile and on all eight tiles around gets smaller as it fades out. Grass sprouts every 30 s on tiles the rabbit can get to (0.1 %, 1 % next to grass; the map may put some on tiles, Tiled's `GRASS` with a `phase` 1–4) and grows like a crop: a tuft and two tufts under everyone, a bush and a big bush over them (90 % opaque), never over the HUD, the action buttons or the wish bubbles. It blocks nothing. |
| On the map, over a field | **The same action button**: over a ripe field it shows the crop; tapping it makes the crop jump out onto a free tile and leaves the field empty but for a tiny sprout (the same for every crop). After picking it shows the watering can: the rabbit waters the field (water falls onto it), it grows a phase at once and then by itself, a phase every 30 s (the plant bounces a little each time and slowly squashes and stretches while it grows), with no button meanwhile; ripe again, the button is back with the crop. The can never runs dry. A sign at the field's top left corner shows its crop; while the field is empty (picked, not yet watered) it shows a question mark instead. Picking a crop makes the face happy (its animation 3 times). Nobody chose what grows yet (no seeds), so after watering a weed may grow instead (40 %, Tiled's `weedChance`); the sign keeps its question mark, and the ripe weed's button pulls it out: it rises a little and fades out, nothing is gained, and the face sweats and says "Beautiful weed but unusable!" for 3 s. |
| On the map, over an animal | **Caring for it** (`CreatureFeature`), on action buttons in a row over the animal. A hand (the pack's paw) strokes it: it stands still while the hand fades out, then a heart over it (not covered by the button; the hand comes back only 20 s after a stroke), a small one (1.5× the pack heart) floats up from just over the player's ears. Whenever it can be fed (standing, or lying: it gets up first; not walking, eating, at night), a second button with its favourite crop (right of the hand; Tiled's `favouriteDish` for the cow, corn for chickens) gives it one (the slot shakes, it eats and shows a heart); without that crop it shakes and the face says "find" with it. Its quest has a third button (`QuestFeature`): `QUEST` with the quest's picture starts it, `DELIVER` with what it waits for gives it (from the quest slot first); when that is its favourite too, only the quest's button shows. With several animals next to the player each button is for one of them and stays over it. |
| On the map | **Quest offers as picture bubbles** (`PictureBubble`): a small grey bubble with a picture over a character (drawn at 3 pack pixels per pixel, its picture as much smaller; a bubble near the top right may sit behind the gear, that is fine), in front of everyone on the map: an animal offering a quest shows what the quest waits for, by day, hidden while the player is next to it (the `QUEST` button is there). Tapping the bubble walks the player next to the animal, as tapping the animal does. Asleep for the night an animal **dreams** of its favourite (`DreamBubble`, a cloud over its "zzz"): shown 3 s, gone 4–8 s, again. |
| Over the backpack, while a quest goes on | **The quest row** (`QuestPanel`, placed by `Hud`, 8 px over the slots): over the first two slots the pack's brown rectangle button (a nine-patch at 3×, 170 × 87) with the asking animal's face (its emoji at 1.5×), an arrow (drawn by `quest_icons.py`, 2×) and what it waits for (3×), and under them the time line (8 px, shrinking from the right: green, yellow from 30 % left, red from 10 %); on the button's top left corner, sticking out 14 px, a small beige round button (3×) with a red X: it cancels the quest. Taps on the button stay in the HUD. Over the third slot the quest slot: a backpack slot tinted golden, with the dark brown shape of what is wanted (3×, the silhouette shader) while empty; the next one of it picked up lands there in its own colours (even with a full backpack), and one carried in the backpack moves there a second after the slot shows empty (its backpack slot shakes, and it flies over into the golden slot, `ItemFlights`); given to the animal, it flies from its slot to the animal on the map (so does a favourite given from the backpack); tapped, it jumps out next to the player. Cancelled or out of time, what is in the slot jumps out (gone with no room). |
| Top left | **The day clock** (`TimeOfDay`, drawn at 80% of the UI's scale, 221 × 120, 24 from the screen's left edge, its middle level with the settings gear's; taps go through it): the weather UI's frame without its label. Its left window shows the part of the day (`DayClock`): the sun by day, the setting sun at dusk, the moon with a star at night, the sun behind a cloud at dawn (the big weather icons). Its right window is the day's scale (the sun at its top, the moon at its bottom) with an arrow over it from the bump on the frame's middle (seven: the pack's five, from about 50° up to 50° down, and two drawn for the game at 80° up and down). It only goes down, clockwise: when the sun starts to rise (dawn) it jumps to nearly straight up (80°), at the sun; then it goes down a step every fifth of the time until sunset; at sunset (dusk) one more step (steeply down); at night nearly straight down (80°) at the moon until sunrise. |
| Top right | **Settings gear** (`HoldButton`): opens only after a 3-second hold, while the pack's stamina circle fills at its top right. The panel (`SettingsPanel`) has only a title and a check mark to close. |

Collecting happens on the map: items lie on tiles, bobbing (`ItemOnGround`), and are picked up by walking onto them.

Changed from the first design: the action is a round button over the thing it acts on, not the face itself; stamina
is a bar, not apples; the day picture is the day clock at the top left (no weather yet); the backpack has 3
slots (2 at first, until 2026‑11‑20).

## Planned

In this order; each step its own branch, scenario tests through `GameDriver` (`e2e/README.md`).

1. **Settings.** Age (3–8, showing what it changes, e.g. "BUTTONS: PICTURE ONLY"), music, sounds. The age decides:
   words on buttons (readers only), talks on or off (off for 3–4), anything else age-dependent (see "Game modes by
   age" in `docs/ideas.md`).
2. **Talks (age 5+), no window.** The HUD slides down (only the gear stays) and the map darkens a little (×0.8). The
   other character's face on the left with its line in a speech bubble (`dialog_box`, text up to ~500 px wide); the
   player's face bottom right with 1–3 short answers in a row (`play_button/blank`, about 10 letters each, enforced by a
   check on the dialog data). A chosen answer and the previous line move up and fade. The last answer has the
   `continue_indicator` and ends the talk: the HUD comes back and a picture bubble over the character sums up what it
   wants. While a talk is open, map taps don't walk.
3. **Age 3–4 hint.** The tool button's hint "play around!" is a placeholder (next to an animal whose favourite or quest's thing the player
   doesn't carry it already says "find" with that crop): when a character wants something, tapping the
   hint shows a big arrow bouncing over the place to go, with a small picture of what is there. Open: what to do when
   that place is off screen (an arrow at the screen edge, or the camera gliding there and back).
4. **A real day.** Weather (the day clock at the top left shows the time of day already), the time for readers, and
   stamina lowered by time and work, refilled by sleep.

Missing models for all this: dialog data (lines, 1–3 answers, which one ends, the summary
picture), the settings and where they are stored, a day clock and weather, portraits for every character that talks.
